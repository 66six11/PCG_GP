Create one polished landscape game environment concept painting for a procedural modular medieval building project. User request: cartoon medieval, reminiscent of the chunky timber, stone and mechanical construction feel of Besiege, but with a much more lively and colorful palette.

Strong warm/cool balance and restrained color grouping; avoid muddy desaturated brown-gray dominance or neon rainbow coloring.
Composition: one cohesive full environment, generously framed, clearly readable architecture at medium scale, wall and staircase details visible in the foreground, central keep as focal silhouette, restrained distant rolling hills and a pale blue sky. Bright warm late-afternoon sunlight, soft cool shadows, gentle atmospheric depth, appealing art-directed lighting. Landscape aspect ratio approximately 16:10. Present it as a finished game art direction key image suitable as a modeling reference.
Constraints: no typography, no labels, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no collage, no asset-sheet panels, no visible grid overlay, no characters obscuring architecture, no modern technology, no battle or destruction.
